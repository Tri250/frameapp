## Feature research and third-party rights

- Examine software you own or are appropriately authorized to analyze. Downloading, purchasing or running it does not automatically authorize reverse engineering, copying or redistribution. Check applicable law, the target's license and service terms; pause the affected analysis if scope is unclear.
- REA's MIT license covers its licensed code, not target applications, commercial analysis tools, fonts, models, images or data.
- Turn lawful observations into inputs, outputs and acceptance criteria, then write original code. Do not contribute third-party decompiled code, proprietary implementations, resource bundles, brand designs, restricted API credentials or private data. Independent implementation is not a guarantee of noninfringement; obtain professional review where needed.
- This project does not provide workflows for cracking, bypassing payment/licensing/DRM, stealing keys, removing copyright notices or accessing services without authorization.

Contributors must have the right to submit code and assets. Record source, version, license and required notices for introduced material; see [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). AI-generated content also needs human review.

## Independent distribution

Distributors decide whether to publish and use their own identifiers, signing, accounts and operator details. They verify privacy, copyright, registration and store requirements. Repository materials are editable examples, not credentials, proof of authorization or a promise of approval. MIT grants no right to impersonate the author or claim endorsement.

Respect privacy, image rights and other rights when taking or processing photos. Changed data handling must match the published policy. Follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for security reports.
